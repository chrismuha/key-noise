Real siren recording credits

56 signal recordings and 33 recorded shutdown tails and 18 authored Test startup/sustain assets for 32 catalog entries. Choose Real sounds and the matching mode. These are field recordings with their own durations, background noise, and recorded wind-downs. Held Real Test uses actual recorded startup, the author’s steady-loop markers, and recorded shutdown at unmodified playback rate; partial re-presses use approximate recorded-position matching. Release and Cancel normally isolate the current recording’s narrow tonal peaks, reject broadband noise, and use a seamless periodic tone with gradual playback-rate and gain changes. Re-powering accelerates the same sample before returning to the paused file. Recorded shutdown companions are a fallback if sampling cannot decode the source. This processed playback is illustrative, not an unedited hardware recording of every possible release point.
