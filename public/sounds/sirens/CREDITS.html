49 signal recordings and 26 recorded shutdown tails for 26 catalog entries. Choose Real sounds and the matching mode. These are field recordings with their own durations, background noise, and recorded wind-downs. Release and Cancel normally process a short sample of the current recording with gradual playback-rate and gain changes. Re-powering accelerates the same sample before returning to the paused file. Recorded shutdown companions are a fallback if sampling cannot decode the source. This processed playback is illustrative, not an unedited hardware recording of every possible release point.

Railroader3921 and Blixt1000 — Redi Wolf publicly dedicated their linked recordings to the public domain. Freesound recordings below use CC0 1.0, CC BY 3.0, or CC BY 4.0, as specified for each file.
